# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the **Tyranox** community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, including customers, everyday users, and technical contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being patient, welcoming, and helpful toward new or non-technical users.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback on products and features.
- Focusing on what is best for users and the community.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Personal attacks, insults, derogatory comments, or harassment of any kind.
- Trolling, spamming, or derailing support and feature discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or close comments, issues, or discussions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the organization maintainers on GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and handled with discretion.
